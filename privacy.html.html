Overview

ApexCareForge is a 100% offline desktop application. We respect your privacy.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app does not connect to the internet, does not use analytics, ads, or tracking.

Local Storage Only

All data you create (care plans, drug cards, SBAR notes, reflections, placement logs) is stored locally on your device in your Documents folder. You control your files.

Permissions

The app requests runFullTrust capability to run as a desktop application and save files locally. It does not access personal data outside its own files.

Children's Privacy

This app is for nursing students and educators. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Third Parties

No third-party services, SDKs, or external data sharing.

Your Rights

You can delete all app data by deleting the app's folder in Documents/ApexCareForge. Uninstalling the app removes the application.

Any changes will be posted here with an updated date.
