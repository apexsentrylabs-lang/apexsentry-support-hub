1. Offline-First - No Cloud

InvoiceForge Pro GLOW is a native Win32 app that stores all invoices 100% offline in a local SQLite vault on your device. No data ever leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Vault: C:\Users\%USERNAME%\AppData\Local\InvoiceForgePro\v5_glow\invoices_glow.db
Logos: C:\Users\%USERNAME%\AppData\Local\InvoiceForgePro\v5_glow\logos\
QR Codes: C:\Users\%USERNAME%\AppData\Local\InvoiceForgePro\v5_glow\qr_*.png

2. What We Collect - Nothing by Default

By default, we collect ZERO personal data. All business info, client info, invoices, amounts, VAT IDs are stored only in your local SQLite file.

Diagnostics (Opt-in Only): If you check "Allow anonymous diagnostics" on first run, we log ONLY:

  • Event types: app_start, save_success, export_pdf, locale_change, qr_generated
  • Never: client names, amounts, invoice content, emails, VAT IDs
  • Stored locally only in diagnostics table - never sent to cloud unless you export logs for support

3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Right to Access: Privacy Center → Export My Data → JSON export of all invoices

Right to Erasure: Privacy Center → Delete All Data → Permanently deletes invoices table (GDPR Art.17)

Right to Portability: Export as JSON or CSV with locale formatting

Legitimate Interest: Local processing under GDPR Art.6(1)(f) - invoice generation on your device

US/Canada/UK Business Ready: Supports US Sales Tax, Canada GST/HST, UK VAT 20%, EU VAT - No Zimbabwe data

4. Languages - Instant Switching

App supports 9 languages embedded, no external files: en-US, en-GB, fr-FR, de-DE, es-ES, zh-CN, ja-JP, ko-KR, hi-IN. Selecting Español (es-ES) instantly switches entire UI to FACTURA #, Añadir Artículo, TOTAL - data preserved, glow flash effect.

5. Glow Edition - Neon UI

GLOW edition uses neon blue #3b82f6 borders, glowing buttons (GlowButton class with pulse), Gaussian blur icon glow, dark theme #050a18 background. No extra permissions required.

6. Third-Party Services - None

No analytics SDK, no ads, no cloud sync, no Stripe/PayPal SDK - payment links are plain URLs you enter (e.g., https://paypal.me/yourname). QR codes generated locally via qrcode library, stored locally.

7. Children's Privacy

App is for business invoicing, not directed to children under 13. No data collected from children.
